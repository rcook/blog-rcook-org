I have started writing a [Double Bass Drumming Training Manual][dbdtm] which is intended
to be a companion to the Bobby Rondinelli and Michael Lauren's
_The Encyclopedia of Double Bass Drumming (Revised Edition)_.
Bear in mind that it naturally takes a lifetime to "master" double bass drumming and that
there's enough material in the original book to last said lifetime. Therefore, it's
likely that it'll take me years to complete this book since I intend to write it as I
progress through it. To give you an example of the pace, I started writing the manual and
going through the book on 2026-07-30 and, thus far, Chapter 1 of the manual (corresponding
to Chapter 1 of the book) is the only fully complete chapter. Chapter 2 is mostly complete
and it's likely that it'll take at least another month for me to complete Chapter 2 of the
and, accordingly, Chapter 2 of the manual. Still, I think there's value in sharing the
manual with the world, particularly for anybody who's starting out.

The source code for the manual is available on [GitHub][gh] and released under the
[Creative Commons Attribution Share Alike 4.0 International][licence]. I invite anyone
who's interest to work on the manual and open [issues][issues] as they find them.

_Note that you will need to own a copy of either the Original or Revised Edition of the
book._
